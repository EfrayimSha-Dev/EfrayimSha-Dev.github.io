Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Purpose

Shoshana TaskManager Backup is a private utility that creates encrypted database backups and stores them in an authorized Google Drive account.

Google Drive Access

Google Drive access is used only to upload, list, verify and manage backup files. The utility does not intentionally access unrelated Google Drive files.

Data Storage and Security

Database backups are encrypted before upload. Google authorization credentials are stored locally on the authorized computer.

Data Sharing

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties by this utility.

Data Retention

Older backup files are deleted according to the configured backup retention policy.

Access Revocation

Google Drive access can be revoked at any time through Google Account security settings. Existing backups can be deleted from Google Drive.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner using the support email displayed on the Google authorization screen.
